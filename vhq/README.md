OpenCode is an additional execution client, not a model. Prefer Codex for complex changes and OpenCode with verified zero-price models (including DeepSeek free variants where available) for bounded tasks. OpenRouter free models are optional after eligibility, privacy and quality checks. Resolve exact model IDs from official catalogs at runtime; allowlist zero-cost routes only, prohibit paid fallback and auto top-up, and fail closed on unknown pricing. Trial credits may be used only with hard no-charge controls and verified expiry/balance. On 429 or exhaustion, persist work and rotate to another authorized free route or queue. Treat generated patches as untrusted and require independent tests. Do not send confidential customer data to free providers without explicit authorization. Implementation and live quota verification pending; no claims of actual connectivity. References: https://opencode.ai/docs/console/models/ and https://openrouter.ai/collections/free-models/.

## Multimodal interaction — owner requirement
Support Arabic RTL text and optional push-to-talk audio input and spoken output for executive and department conversations, meetings, task intake and reporting. Preserve editable transcripts and structured task records; low-confidence speech must be reviewed before submission. Voice is not authentication or authorization; sensitive, paid, destructive or production actions require the same explicit authenticated approval as text. No always-on microphone by default. Obtain participant consent before recording meetings; do not retain raw audio by default. Keep a text-only fallback. Start with browser speech recognition/synthesis where available, disclose potential third-party processing; evaluate lightweight local whisper.cpp for privacy and Yemeni dialect accuracy on 8 GB RAM. No additional paid service without approval. Voice support is planned, not implemented or verified. Acceptance: Arabic and Yemeni dialect test corpus, transcript correction, permission denial, unsupported browser, interruption, duplicate submission prevention, and no dispatch from unconfirmed transcripts.
